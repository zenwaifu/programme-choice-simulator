Model Documentation & Assumptions

  • Decision maker: Aiman, selecting one programme from 6 mutually exclusive alternatives.
  • Utility function: U_i = Σ (weight_j × rating_ij) — weighted linear combination of factor ratings.
  • Choice rule — MNL: Probabilities via softmax:
P(i) = exp(μ × U_i) / Σⱼ exp(μ × U_j)
  • Scale factor μ: Controls decisiveness. Higher μ concentrates probability on top-utility option. Default = 2.5.
  • IIA assumption: MNL assumes Independence of Irrelevant Alternatives — ratio of any two probabilities is unaffected by other alternatives.
  • Ratings: Integer scale 1–5 (1 = very low, 5 = very high), adjustable per programme per factor.
  • Weights: Reflect importance of each factor (default: Interest 30%, Career 25%, Exam 20%, Location 10%, Fees 10%, Explore 5%).
  • Stochastic element: Even the lowest-utility programme retains a nonzero selection probability.
